# Experience

I'm a full-stack developer with equal amounts of interest in user interaction and making the backend tick like a well oiled machine, because they are one and the same. In my experience content is the king: if it doesn't display, or displays wrongly, it doesn't matter how fancy the UI effects are.

Linux I've used for a long time and am perfectly comfortable with it. Its myriad of window managers is where I still draw my inspiration for UX/UI work. I know what works and what doesn't.

Then I explored a lot of different programming languages during my university years. Java, JS, TS, Python, Haskell, Scheme, Common Lisp, Clojure, Ada.. Always looking for ways to do things faster and better.

Then I took the hard route and went into AI track, that didn't work out all the way to Master's, but managed to write my B.Sc. thesis about deep neural networks, so among other things I know what makes the LLM's tick.

Then I morphed into a modern fullstack developer during a university Capstone project which my team made as a mobile-first Angular single-page application with nosql MongoDB backend.

Been on that road ever since. Some xp I've picked up along the way in no particular order: JavaEE, Spring Boot, Micronaut, Angular, React, Vue, Postgres, Docker, AWS, Flyway, Maven, Gradle, Node, Htmx, Tailwind, Bootstrap, Fiber, Express, Hibernate, Thymeleaf, JSP.

I maintain my career-relevant work history on LinkedIn.
